How a project runs

  1. 1

    Brief

    You describe what you need. A few paragraphs are enough; I will ask about the rest.

  2. 2

    Scope and quote

    I reply with questions, then a written scope with a fixed price and a timeline before any work starts.

  3. 3

    Build with previews

    You follow progress in a repository and on a preview link, and review each milestone.

  4. 4

    Handover

    You receive the source code, setup documentation and help with deployment to your infrastructure.
